How It Works

From chaos to zero wait
in 4 steps

No more overcrowded waiting areas. Customers join remotely and arrive exactly when it's their turn — every time.

🗺️
1
Discover & Join
Customer opens SalonQ, finds your salon on the live map, and joins the queue via QR scan or remotely from home.
🤖
2
AI Estimates Wait
AI analyses queue depth, staff speed, and 30 past service logs to give an accurate ETA with confidence score.
🔔
3
Alert Sent at 15 Min
Customer shops, eats, or rests nearby. Gets a free push notification 15 minutes before their turn — no charges.
✂️
4
Walk In, Get Served
Customer arrives at the perfect moment, scans QR to confirm arrival, and gets seated immediately — zero extra wait.

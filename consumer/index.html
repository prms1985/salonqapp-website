Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the SalonQ Consumer app free to use?
Yes! SalonQ is 100% free for consumers. There are no hidden charges, booking fees, or notification costs.
How does the AI wait time prediction work?
Our AI engine continuously analyzes the average service duration of salon stylists across their last 30 completed log entries, current queue length, and walk-in arrivals to give an accurate ETA with a live confidence percentage.
Can I join the queue without downloading the app?
Yes, if you are physically at the salon, you can scan the salon entrance QR code using your phone camera to check into the queue without downloading. However, installing the Consumer App lets you join queues remotely from home and receive push alerts when your turn is near!
What if I get delayed and can't arrive in time?
You can easily delay your turn or cancel your queue token directly in the app with one tap so the next customer can be served without delay.
